if a day needs explaining, the explanation goes in `context/ledger-notes/YYYY-MM.md`
under that date, and the row here just says what happened. A row that grows into a
paragraph is a row nobody rereads and a file that costs a fortune to load.

Reading it: `tail -n 8 context/ledger.md` for the recent week. Never read it whole.
