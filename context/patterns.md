# Failure patterns

Written by the weekly review. Evidence required — cite ledger dates.

## P1 — Selective dropping, not effort collapse

Effort does not collapse; the audience-facing half gets cancelled while client
work continues. Evidence: 2026-08-24 (content shoot cancelled, client blocks
continued, 5.95h/14.7h = 40%); 2026-08-24 (all three content tasks for the week
DELETED, first zero-content week); 2026-08-26 (Scripnals review and course-plan
talk both dropped, client download kept). The thing that gets cut is always the
thing with no external deadline.

## P2 — The debt gets paid with the body

Work not done on the planned day is repaid in a single death-march session.
Evidence: 5.95h against 14.7h planned on 2026-08-24, repaid 2026-08-25 as a
19h06m span ending 1:42am with 16.23h logged focus and no nap. The march WORKED —
the deliverable shipped — which is the most dangerous lesson available. Watch for
any day whose logged focus exceeds 12h; that is not a good day, it is an invoice.

## P3 — Money booked is not money moved (candidate, 2026-08-26)

Not yet a weekly-review finding — seeded from the June–August budget sheets at the
five-domain build. The sheets booked ₦900,250 to Savings and Emergency across three
months. ₦305,000 survives, as the Cowrywise investment. ₦595,250 is gone, and the
August building payment dropped from ₦500,000 to ₦200,000 to cover it. The same
shape as P1: the line with no external party watching it is the line that gets cut,
whether it is a content shoot or a savings transfer. Confirm or kill this at the
first money-week review.
