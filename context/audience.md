# Audience

Content, course, community, mentorship, Scripnals — everything audience-facing.

## SMART goal

**One high-quality video published Wednesday 7:00pm WAT, every week, to Instagram
and TikTok (same video, both platforms).**

Stated target: **5,000 followers by end of year.**

**5,000 is an AMBITION, not a SMART goal.** 460 → 5,000 on Instagram in four months
is roughly 11x. At one post a week that is very unlikely, and it fails the R in
SMART. His goal stays in his file — but the SMART version underneath it is the one
that gets scored:

> **18 consecutive Wednesdays without a miss, between now and 31 December.**

That is the thing he controls. Followers are the thing he does not.

## The one lever

**Depth over volume. The cadence is the point, not the count.** His own reading of
his own posted content: reach follows focus, not frequency.

## Weekly minimum

One video published, **on the day, at the time**. Thursday is a missed week.

## Cost of breaking it

No YouTube for 2 days (`context/stakes.md`). **Missing the TIME counts as missing
the week.** Self-enforced, therefore currently unenforced — see stakes.md.

## How it is verified

The post exists at the URL by 7:00pm Wednesday. Not "it's rendering", not "it's
uploading". Live, or missed.

## Content pipeline

Publish day contains zero production. The video is banked Tuesday.

| Day | Phase | Time | Block |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thursday | Ideation | 1h | 2:00–3:00pm |
| Friday | Scripting | 2h | 2:00–4:00pm |
| Saturday | Filming | 3h | 2:00–5:00pm |
| Monday | Editing | 3h | 2:00–5:00pm |
| Tuesday | Finish + BANK IT | 2h | 2:00–4:00pm |
| **Wednesday** | **PUBLISH 7:00pm — banked, zero work** | — | 6:45–7:00pm |

**Blocks moved from morning to afternoon on 2026-08-26 at Samuel's instruction.**
His reason: client edits own his mornings almost every day; morning content only
happens on days with no client project.

The objection was stated and overruled: the afternoon is exactly where the tiebreak
gets lost, which was the whole reason for the morning slot. **The test — if the
afternoon content block is eaten by client work twice in one week, the override was
wrong and it goes back to mornings.** The weekly review states the count every week.

Saturday and Monday are 3h against a 2h window, so they take the first hour of
client block 2. That is planned, not a surprise.
